How It Works

1. Set Your Profile

Enter your health conditions, allergies, and dietary preferences.

2. Log Your Inventory

Tell us what you already have in your kitchen. The system tracks your pantry stock.

3. Get Smart Recommendations

Receive Nigerian recipe suggestions ranked by how well they match your pantry and health needs.

4. Plan Your Week

Select meals for each day of the week from your personalized recommendations.

5. Generate Shopping List

The system compares your meal plan against inventory and outputs only what you need to buy.

Built for the Nigerian Context

Unlike global platforms, NaijaMeal understands local staples — from Egusi to Ofada Rice, from Swallows to Pepper Soup. The constraint engine enforces your health rules before any recommendation is shown.
